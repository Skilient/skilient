## Questions

- Q1 (approved 2026-11-28, Ahmed; done): the no-foreign-schema lint flags any string with `<schema>.<letter>`, case-insensitive, and so flags two strings in notify that are not queries: web's wording "Your verified CV was refreshed with this month's work. It has a new code." (`src/domain/describe.ts`; it must stay verbatim) and the config key `ranking.formula` (`src/domain/notices.ts` and its unit test). I did not bypass or reshape around the rule. Recommendation: make the rule's identifier after the dot case-sensitive and require no space after the dot (`work. It` is prose), with a lint unit test for both, and allow `ranking.formula` as a known config key in services other than ranking (a short allowlist of config keys in `tools/lint/src/schemas.mjs`). Done: the rule is tightened as recommended, with lint tests.
- Q2 (approved 2026-11-28, Ahmed): the brief asks for "the tier-up notice from `tier.changed`", but the stack has no tier notification (no type in `notification_types`, no wording in `describe.ts`). Notify handles `tier.changed` and writes nothing. If a tier-up notice is wanted, it needs a type, a category and wording first.
- Q3 (approved 2026-11-28, Ahmed): the stack's push functions called `require_user()`, which also refused before onboarding finished. Notify has no onboarding fact (it is people's); web's gate keeps un-onboarded users in onboarding, so push settings are refused only when signed out.

- Q4 (pending Ahmed): Azure's SPF check wants skilient.com's SPF to end in `-all`; it ends in `~all` (Zoho's). Hardening it affects every sender of skilient.com mail, including production's Brevo until the cutover. Recommendation: switch to `-all` after the cutover, when Brevo is off, or now if Brevo's sends are DKIM-aligned (they pass DMARC without our SPF). Until SPF verifies, the domain isn't linked to `skilient-comms` and no Azure email can go out.
