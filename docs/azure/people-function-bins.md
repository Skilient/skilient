# People: function tracking sheet (W2)

Date: 2026-11-25. Branch: `azure`. Source: `apps/web/supabase/migrations/` (83 files). Format as in `docs/azure/identity-function-bins.md`.

Every SQL function the `people` service owns, in one of the five bins from "How each function is ported" in `docs/azure/prd.md`, with its caller, the people tables it touches, and its golden case. Goldens were recorded before the port (`packages/testing/test/people.golden.test.ts` → `packages/testing/test/golden/people/*.json`, 24 cases, recorded on the local stack and compared twice) and replayed against the port (`services/people/test/golden/*.golden.test.ts`, all match). The people halves of identity's goldens (`signup.json`, `deletion.json`) are replayed end to end in `services/people/test/golden/identity-halves.golden.test.ts`.

**Boundary.** The PRD service catalogue (decisions 2026-11-25, Ahmed; W2 Q4 and Q5, advisor): people owns profiles and every privacy setting, universities, domains, departments and programmes, university requests, friends, blocks, mutes, suggestion dismissals, onboarding, tours, tips and small UI state, the page shell, people search and suggestions, the graduation rule. Identity owns accounts and the deletion state (people mirrors `deleting`); market owns university seats, claims and the recruiter preferences; content, messaging, work, ranking, community, credentials and notify own what the shell counts (people keeps event-fed copies, decision Q1).

**People tables on the current stack** (all in `public`): `profiles`, `profiles_public_card`, `onboarding_state`, `universities`, `university_domains`, `university_requests`, `departments`, `programmes`, `friend_requests`, `friendships`, `blocks`, `user_mutes`, `pymk_dismissals`, `ui_state`, `tour_progress`, `tips_seen`. In the port (`services/people/migrations/`): 17 tables in `0001_people.sql` (the card folded into `profiles`, plus `audit_log` and `deleted_accounts`) and 5 event-fed copies in `0002_copies.sql`.

Locations are the latest definition, as `file:line` under `apps/web/supabase/migrations/`; app callers under `apps/web/`. Wrappers generated by `pg_temp.expose` point at that call.
