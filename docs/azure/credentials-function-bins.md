Every SQL function the `credentials` service inherits, in one of the five bins from "How each function is ported" in `docs/azure/prd.md`, with its app caller, what replaces it and its golden case. The list was generated by rule (every `create function` in the four files; callers by name in `apps/web/{lib,app,components,supabase/functions}`) and each row was then placed by hand.

**Boundary.** The PRD service catalogue and the W8 brief: credentials owns the Record (entries with their signed payload, envelope hash and signature; claims by email hash; share links by token hash; corrections; officer confirm, correct, decline; revoke by officers or staff; students' Record, display, mistakes, claims and waiting count; anyone's verify of an entry or a transcript and a share link), CVs (CvSnapshotV1, settings, signed versions, issue and re-issue, the monthly refresh, signing keys and the public key file, share links, views, verify, revoke, PDF exports with the MAC check), and the signing itself. Not credentials': the ranking formula's Record component (ranking, W6, already ported from `record.sealed` and `record.revoked`), appeals and reports on entries (trust, W10), the claim email and every notice (notify, W9, from credentials' events), files (media, W9), badges on the CV (market, W11: `awards` is `[]` until market's batch read exists).

**Inputs that are other services' data.** The snapshot's skills, projects, open-source work, endorsements and credentials are work's: work's new `CvFacts` builds them in work's SQL (the same queries as `cv_snapshot_base` and the teacher-portal layer), with endorsers as `$name:` placeholders filled from people. The person, university and leaderboard choice are people's (`ProfileCards`, which gains `onboarding_complete` and `leaderboard_opt_out`); the address and a ban are identity's (`EmailsOf`, new); the tier and percentile are ranking's (`TiersOf`, which gains `percentile`). Record entries come from `event.closed` and `society.term_changed` (community; titles, labels, walk-ins' addresses and society names through community's new `RecordFacts`), `venture.completed` (titles through work's new `VentureTitles`), `task.completed` and `swap.closed` (work; `swap.closed` gains the catalogue `skill_label`).
