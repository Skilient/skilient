# Content: function tracking sheet (W3)

Date: 2026-11-26. Branch: `azure`. Source: `apps/web/supabase/migrations/` (83 files), mainly `20260929010000_posts.sql`, `20260929020000_comments_links.sql`, `20260929030000_micro_survey.sql`, `20260929040000_feed_ranking.sql`, `20261115000001_feed_two_step.sql`, `20261115000000_cron_load.sql`, `20261114000003_share_reach_push.sql`, `20261019000000_uni_comms.sql`, `20261111000001_societies.sql`, `20260930000000_phase3_answers.sql`, `20261104000000_analytics.sql`, `20261010000000_student_portal.sql`, `20261018000000_uni_ecosphere.sql`, `20261005000000_ranking.sql`, and the Edge Function logic in `apps/web/supabase/functions/_shared/links/` and `functions/link-preview`. Format as in `docs/azure/people-function-bins.md`.

Every SQL function the `content` service owns, in one of the five bins from "How each function is ported" in `docs/azure/prd.md`, with its caller, the content tables it touches, and its golden case. Goldens were recorded before the port (`packages/testing/test/content.golden.test.ts` → `packages/testing/test/golden/content/*.json`, 25 cases in 6 files, compared twice on the local stack) and replayed against the port (`services/content/test/golden/*.golden.test.ts`: 24 of 24 content cases match; share.json is messaging's to replay in W4). Wrappers are skipped by decision 2026-11-25 and their private function's golden covers them.

**Boundary.** The PRD service catalogue gives `content`: posts, comments, polls, link previews, micro-survey, views, the ranked feed, sharing (events `post.created`, `post.removed`, `comment.created`, `survey.answered`). Also content: post media and venture-update media, post events and RSVPs on posts, hides, stats, feed sessions, and university announcements as posts (the admin permission is market's; society "post as" is community's check on content's `create_post`). People owns `user_mutes` (already in `people-function-bins.md`; content reads it as an event-fed copy). Messaging owns the chat message of a share. Names repeat: `private.rsvp_event(p_post uuid, p_status rsvp_status)` is content's, `private.rsvp_event(p_id uuid)` is community's; rows are keyed by full signature where a name repeats.

**Content tables on the current stack** (all in `public`): `posts`, `post_media`, `post_events`, `event_rsvps`, `post_polls`, `poll_options`, `poll_votes`, `venture_update_media`, `post_comments`, `post_hides`, `link_previews`, `micro_survey_dimensions`, `micro_survey_questions`, `micro_survey_assignments`, `micro_survey_responses`, `post_views`, `post_survey_counts`, `post_stats`, `feed_sessions`, `announcement_meta`, `announcement_targets` (21). Not content's: `platform_config` (see Unclear), `university_hides`, `user_mutes`.

Locations are the latest definition, as `file:line` under `apps/web/supabase/migrations/`; app callers under `apps/web/`. Wrappers generated by `pg_temp.expose` point at that call.
